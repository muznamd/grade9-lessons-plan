Grade 9 Teacher Lesson Plans

Comprehensive Overview: Units 1, 2 & 3

Unit 1: Lifestyle (Grade 9)

Lesson 1 – Reading

Focus: Daily routines, health habits, and skimming skills

Aims: Read texts about habits around the world, extract specific details, and discuss personal lifestyles.

Key Activities: Matching headings to paragraphs, true/false comprehension check, vocabulary identification.

Lesson 2 – Grammar

Focus: Present simple and present continuous

Aims: Differentiate between habitual actions and temporary situations happening now using correct auxiliary verbs.

Key Activities: Timeline sorting, gap-fill drills, and targeted speaking exchanges.

Lesson 3 – Vocabulary

Focus: Free-time activities, hobbies, and collocations

Aims: Learn and apply common verb-noun collocations related to hobbies and sports (play, do, go).

Key Activities: Word mapping, survey creation, and matching words with definitions.

Lesson 4 – Listening

Focus: Listening for specific information and opinions

Aims: Comprehend short dialogues about daily routines and leisure choices.

Key Activities: Multiple-choice listening tasks, note-taking, and identifying speaker attitudes.

Lesson 5 – Speaking

Focus: Expressing preferences and asking questions

Aims: Talk about likes, dislikes, and frequencies using adjectives of frequency (always, sometimes, never).

Key Activities: Information-gap pair work, structured interviews, and reporting back to the class.

Lesson 6 – Writing

Focus: Descriptive paragraph writing about routines

Aims: Write a cohesive personal profile paragraph using appropriate linking words (and, but, because, so).

Key Activities: Sentence building, paragraph structuring, and peer review.

Lesson 7 – Switch On

Focus: Digital lifestyles, screen time, and media habits

Aims: Analyze short video segments on modern technology usage and balance.

Key Activities: Video comprehension questions, class discussion panel, and habit tracking.

Lesson 8 – Unit Check

Focus: Unit 1 comprehensive consolidation

Aims: Review target grammar structures, vocabulary sets, and reading skills learned across Unit 1.

Key Activities: Interactive quiz game, self-assessment checklist, and targeted practice.

Unit 2: Connections (Grade 9)

Lesson 1 – Reading

Focus: Understanding main points, minority languages

Aims: Read short texts/notices, answer multiple-choice details, discuss saving minority languages.

Key Activities: True/false quiz on languages (Zulu, Inuktitut, Gaelic, Maori, Welsh), looking for synonyms.

Lesson 2 – Grammar

Focus: Past simple & past continuous, stative verbs

Aims: Use past continuous with was/were + -ing, recognise stative verbs (understand, want, love).

Key Activities: Explore grammar boxes, audio practice, personalised past-action discussions.

Lesson 3 – Vocabulary

Focus: Language verbs & verb-preposition collocations

Aims: Master communication verbs (say, tell, speak, talk) and collocations (listen to, explain to).

Key Activities: Dictionary work, multiple-choice cloze practice task.

Lesson 4 – Listening

Focus: Predicting info, -ing forms as subjects

Aims: Sentence/note completion, animal communication talk, role-play interview.

Key Activities: Predicting missing word types, managing distractors in audio.

Lesson 5 – Speaking

Focus: Fluency, individual long turn (photos)

Aims: Speak at length with few pauses, organise descriptions (place, people, clothes, background).

Key Activities: Recording self-assessments, structured photo comparison pairs.

Lesson 6 – Writing

Focus: Social media posts, strong adjectives & adverbs

Aims: Write a 100-word narrative story using strong modifiers (brilliant, hilarious, absolutely).

Key Activities: Model analysis, peer editing, chronological paragraph planning.

Lesson 7 – Switch On

Focus: Phone habits, pronunciation of I / I'd / I'll

Aims: Video interviews analysis, group project on 1980s communication.

Key Activities: Contraction drills, historical tech role-play presentation.

Lesson 8 – Unit Check

Focus: Unit 2 consolidation & review

Aims: Review past tenses, communication verbs, open cloze practice.

Key Activities: Interactive review exercises, self-reflection summary tasks.

Unit 3: The Future Is Now (Grade 9)

Lesson 1 – Reading

Focus: Skim-reading, AI and entertainment future

Aims: Complete gapped texts with missing sentences, learn vocabulary (transform, lifelike, consume).

Key Activities: Skimming techniques, linking-word analysis, futuristic entertainment brainstorming.

Lesson 2 – Grammar

Focus: Talking about the future (will, going to, present continuous)

Aims: Match future forms to intentions, predictions, arrangements, and scheduled events.

Key Activities: Inductive grammar sorting, audio verification, personal schedule dialogues.

Lesson 3 – Vocabulary

Focus: Household technology & phrasal verbs

Aims: Name appliances (microwave, dishwasher, air conditioning), master separable phrasal verbs.

Key Activities: Appliance labelling, multiple-choice cloze, smart-home problem solving.

Lesson 4 – Listening

Focus: Picture-based listening, advice & suggestions

Aims: Identify differences in pictures, use *should, ought to, why don't you* for technology advice.

Key Activities: Distractor spotting, information-gap tech troubleshooting role-play.

Lesson 5 – Speaking

Focus: Collaborative tasks, opinions, agreeing/disagreeing

Aims: Politeness strategies in discussion, choosing AI assistants collaboratively.

Key Activities: Useful language function grouping, robot design presentations.

Lesson 6 – Writing

Focus: Informal emails, expressing concern & giving advice

Aims: Write a well-structured technology support response email to a peer.

Key Activities: Model email analysis, paragraph planning, self-assessment checklist.

Lesson 7 – Switch On

Focus: Healthcare technology (telemedicine, 3D printing)

Aims: Watch documentaries for key details, design an innovative health tech device.

Key Activities: Vocabulary matching in context, group project pitch with reasons.

Lesson 8 – Unit Check

Focus: Units 1–3 comprehensive review

Aims: Consolidate future forms, phrasal verbs, appliances, and open cloze text execution.

Key Activities: 20 questions game, listening verification checkpoints.
